You found YggMesh — a new kind of Internet 🌐

YggMesh connects nearby routers into a decentralized wireless mesh.

Nearby routers discover each other automatically and route traffic across the mesh — without relying on a central server or cellular network.

🔍

Cell network down? Blackout in the district? As long as at least one node has global connectivity, everyone does.
